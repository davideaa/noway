## Copione (inglese, per voce e testo a schermo)
1. America is pumping more oil than ever. On track for a record 13.8 million barrels a day.
2. So why is gas still $4.47?
3. Because crude is only one part of the price.
4. Every gallon pays for three things: the crude, the refinery, and taxes plus the trip to your tank.
5. Crude got pricier. WTI averaged $65 in 2025. This year, $84. This week, it hit $99.
6. But the real squeeze is the refinery.
7. Think of crude as flour. Gasoline is the bread.
8. The world has plenty of flour. It's short on bakeries.
9. Refineries in Russia, China and the Middle East have been disrupted.
10. So U.S. refineries are running at 97%. Almost flat out.
11. And diesel just hit $6.29 a gallon. A record in dollar terms.
12. Diesel moves trucks, trains and tractors. So it ends up in the price of almost everything.
13. Record oil doesn't mean cheap fuel. The bottleneck isn't the well. It's the refinery.

Nota: le tre parti del prezzo nel bicchiere sono disegnate **non in scala** (scritto a schermo).
